**Logline:** One button each. Hold to fly straight, let go to circle. Last moth glowing wins.

**Description (paste):**
Mothlight is a one-button party game for 1-4 players on one keyboard or one phone. Every moth circles on its own.
Hold your button and it flies straight, and that is the only control. Eat drifting motes of light to grow your glowing trail.
If you touch any trail, even your own, you burn out. So the more you eat, the less room you have to circle, and the
moth that was winning is suddenly the one in danger. After 18 seconds the dark starts closing in, and in overtime every trail
grows by itself, so every round has to end. The last moth glowing takes the round, and the first to three wins.
Party mode: each player presses their own key (A, L, V, ↑) to join, or holds their own corner of a touchscreen.
Empty seats are filled by computer moths, so you can also play solo. Music and sound are arranged live in the browser from recorded CC0 samples (VSCO 2 Community Edition instruments, Kenney SFX), and the
groove gets busier as the arena shrinks.
